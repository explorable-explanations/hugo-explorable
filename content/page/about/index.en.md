Explorable Explanations is run by Yuichi Yazaki, a data visualization specialist.

## Profile

Yuichi holds a master's degree in Design Science from Chiba Institute of Technology's graduate school. He spent seven years as a UI designer and art director at Business Architects Inc. before going independent. Since then, he has been active in data visualization practice and outreach through corporate and academic training and lectures (including Sony, Nikon, and NTT Communications), teaching at universities and graduate schools (including Tama Art University, Musashino Art University, and Digital Hollywood University Graduate School), and running the "Data Visualization Japan" community.

Founder of Data Visualization Japan. Yahoo! News Expert.
