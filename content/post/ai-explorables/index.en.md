AI Explorables is a collection of interactive explainers about AI and machine learning from Google PAIR. It turns abstract topics such as model behavior, datasets, bias, privacy, and text generation into experiences you can explore directly.

Each piece lets you change inputs or conditions with sliders, choices, and visualized data distributions, then see how the results respond. It is a useful entry point for learning the relationship between models and data without treating AI as a black box.
