Much of the content introduced and localized on Explorable Explanations was created by researchers and developers outside Japan. This page lists, for each article, the original creator or source and the license information we were able to confirm.

## About this site's articles

The commentary written by Yuichi Yazaki (learning objectives, walkthroughs, background sections, and so on) is published under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/), the same as the rest of this site. This applies only to the writing on this site — the third-party tools introduced or embedded below remain under whatever license their original creators have set.
